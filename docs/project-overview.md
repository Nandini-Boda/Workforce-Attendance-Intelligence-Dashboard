# Workforce Attendance Intelligence Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview

The Workforce Attendance Intelligence Dashboard is a business intelligence solution designed to provide centralized visibility into workforce attendance, punctuality, working hours, and employee attendance patterns.

The dashboard supports HR teams and management by bringing organization-level attendance reporting and individual employee insights into one analytical experience.

## 2. Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to help users:

- Monitor overall workforce attendance.
- Understand attendance patterns across departments.
- Review present, absent, leave, and weekly-off distributions.
- Monitor punctuality and working hours.
- Investigate individual employee attendance history.
- Identify attendance trends across selected periods.
- Support structured HR and management reporting.

## 3. Intended Users

- HR teams
- Department managers
- Workforce operations teams
- Business leaders
- Management reporting stakeholders

## 4. Dashboard Experiences

### Attendance Overview

The organization-level dashboard provides attendance KPIs, department comparisons, attendance distributions, date-based trends, and an employee summary directory.

### Employee Insights

The employee-level dashboard provides individual attendance statistics, a monthly attendance calendar, daily punch history, working-hour summaries, and punctuality indicators.

## 5. Data and Reporting Approach

The dashboard uses Google BigQuery as its workforce attendance data source. Attendance information is processed into reporting categories and business metrics for presentation through the dashboard.

Date filters and employee or department selections help users explore the information at different levels.

## 6. Business Value

The solution is designed to support:

- More accessible workforce attendance information.
- Consistent attendance reporting.
- Easier department-level comparisons.
- Better visibility into punctuality and working hours.
- Faster employee-level attendance reviews.
- More informed HR and management discussions.

## 7. Project Scope

The current dashboard focuses on attendance overview and employee-level insights.

Potential future enhancements include leave-balance reporting, performance analytics, and downloadable executive reports.

## 8. Data Privacy

This documentation intentionally excludes confidential employee information, private database identifiers, credentials, and internal organizational details. Public portfolio materials should use sanitized or demonstration information.
